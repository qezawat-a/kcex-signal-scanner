# Swing playbook (1h / 4h / 1d)

- Prefer signals where 1h + 4h EMA trend agree; 15m is entry timing only.
- Require RSI confirmation: LONG needs RSI(14) > 55 on 1h, SHORT needs < 45.
- MACD histogram must have the right sign on 4h (no counter-trend entries against 4h MACD).
- Position framing: SL = 2×ATR(4h), TP1 = 2×ATR, TP2 = 3.5×ATR. State both targets in the report.
- Max 2 concurrent symbol ideas; rotate to the highest-confidence one when a new scan beats it by 10+ points.
